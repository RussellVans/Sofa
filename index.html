Sofa AR Demo

Turn the sofa, pick a fabric, then place it at true size in your own room.

Tap the button, then move the phone slowly over the floor until the surface is found. Tap to place the sofa. It appears at 200 × 90 × 86 cm, and you can walk around it.

Fit it in your room

Start the live camera and point it at the spot where the sofa would go. Drag the sofa into place, then use the sliders for size and turn.

Drag the sofa into place, then tap Lock in place so it stays on that spot when you turn the phone. Hold the phone upright and stay where you are: it follows turning, not walking, and the size is approximate. For a sofa fixed on the floor at true size while you walk around it, use View in your space above.
